    What this model can and cannot say

    One person; tasks are available from the start and divisible into interchangeable 30-minute units. No prerequisites, travel time, fatigue or uncertainty. Enter remaining effort, not work already completed. Dates are civil dates in one time basis: no timezone or daylight-saving conversion. End times and deadlines may use 24:00. Intervals must not overlap within each list.
